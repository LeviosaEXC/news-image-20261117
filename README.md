Built for make.fun's **news-image-20261117 "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image"** bounty: a single-page newspaper image for Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

## The image

**"THE WORLD IN ONE DAY"** — a front page with a masthead and dateline, a lead story on the Kerch Bridge, a 2×2 grid of the day's other big stories (Kyiv, the Gulf, Shenzhen, jets), a "BY THE NUMBERS" strip, and a news ticker. Every number is captioned with its source; every headline is drawn, no AI imagery.

## The entry

- `entry.png` — the submitted image (1024×1024 JPEG).
- `main.ts` — serves the image.
